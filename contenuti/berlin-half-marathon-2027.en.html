The race

Why run it

One of the fastest half marathons in Europe: a flat course (just 4.3 metres between its lowest and highest points) on very wide roads in the heart of Berlin, from the Victory Column to the Brandenburg Gate, past Charlottenburg Palace, the Kurfürstendamm, Potsdamer Platz, the Gendarmenmarkt and Alexanderplatz.

In 2026 more than 40,000 runners from 134 countries signed up, with around 40 bands and cheering points along the route. The course records say it all: 58:42 for men (Eric Kiptanui, 2018) and 63:35 for women (Fotyen Tesfay, 2025).

It is run in spring with ideal temperatures and it is the German stage of the SuperHalfs circuit: perfect for chasing a personal best or for discovering the city on the run.

The course

The course and the weather

The start is on Straße des 17. Juni, between the Victory Column and the Brandenburg Gate, and the finish is just behind the Brandenburg Gate. In between is a loop of the city on wide boulevards: west to Charlottenburg Palace (km 3) and the Kurfürstendamm (km 6), then back towards the centre via Potsdamer Platz (km 12), the Gendarmenmarkt (km 15) and Alexanderplatz (km 17.5), the “highest” point, before the run-in to the Gate. The maximum difference in height is 4.3 metres: practically a track.

Refreshments: water and tea at km 5, 9.8, 14.1 and 17.5 (with bananas at km 14.1 and 17.5 and Maurten drink at km 5 and 17.5), Maurten gels at km 12.3 and water only at km 19.5; at the finish, water, tea and fruit. The time limit is 3 hours 15 minutes.

For 2027 the organiser lists the runners' start from 10:05, in waves based on the time given at registration; inline skaters start first (9:15), followed by wheelchair and handbike athletes.

In early April the day starts at around 4-7 degrees and reaches 11-14 in the afternoon, with a chance of light rain and wind between the buildings. Coach's tips: dress in layers, keep a dry top for after the race, don't get carried away by the pack in the first 3 kilometres and save something for the final straight towards the Brandenburg Gate.

How it works

The weekend programme

Friday 2 April: arrival in Berlin, check-in at the B&B Hotel Berlin-Alexanderplatz and first contact with KiRun assistance on site.

Saturday 3 April: bib collection at the expo in the former Tempelhof airport with our staff, who go with you and explain the logistics; group shakeout run with the KiRun coach in the centre, free time in the city and the pre-race briefing at the KiRun desk in the hotel, covering kit, pacing and access to the start pens.

Sunday 4 April: breakfast at the hotel, walk or metro to the start area at the Brandenburg Gate, the race and the KiRun meeting point at the finish to celebrate together.

Monday 5 April: breakfast and home, or an extra night if you want to visit Berlin at a slower pace.

Berlin

First time in Berlin? Here's what to expect

Berlin is big, green and surprisingly easy to get around, and from the hotel in Mitte the best of it is within walking distance. The classic first walk starts at the Brandenburg Gate, your finish line on Sunday: close by are the Reichstag, whose glass dome you can visit for free (book online on the Bundestag website a few weeks ahead), and the Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe.

Along Unter den Linden you reach Museum Island, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the Cathedral; at Alexanderplatz, next to the hotel, the TV Tower gives you the highest view over the city. To understand the story of the Wall there is the East Side Gallery, more than 1.3 km of Wall along the Spree painted in 1990 by 118 artists from 21 countries, the Bernauer Straße memorial and Checkpoint Charlie.

On Saturday, after collecting your bib at Tempelhof, take a walk on the Tempelhofer Feld: the runways of the former airport are now a huge park, perfect for loosening your legs. After the finish, recover the Berlin way: currywurst or döner, a beer (alcohol-free works too) in a beer garden if the sun is out, and an evening in Kreuzberg or Prenzlauer Berg.

Two practical tips: shops are closed on Sundays (bars, restaurants, museums and station shops stay open), and a day ticket or multi-trip ticket is the easiest option for the U-Bahn and S-Bahn; cards are accepted almost everywhere, but some cash is handy in smaller places.

The trip

Getting there and around

Berlin can be reached by plane from Italy, direct or with one stop depending on your departure airport: you can book the flight yourself and send us the details, or ask us to add it to the package. Berlin Brandenburg airport is south-east of the city and is connected to the centre by regional trains and the S-Bahn, with convenient stops for Alexanderplatz.

In the city you get around by U-Bahn, S-Bahn, tram and on foot: the hotel is on Voltairestraße, a few minutes' walk from Alexanderplatz, so on race morning you reach the start and finish area near the Brandenburg Gate without stress, and after the finish you are back in your room in a few minutes by metro.

Your questions

FAQ

Is the bib guaranteed?

Yes: registration is open and the bib from the KiRun allocation is added to the package price, while places last. The price is in the price list above.

Where do I collect my bib?

At the expo in the former Tempelhof airport, open on Friday 2 April from 9 am to 8 pm and Saturday 3 April from 9 am to 7 pm. You collect it in person, with the start card the organiser emails a few days before and a photo ID; it cannot be collected on Sunday. We go together on Saturday.

Can I leave a bag at the start?

The organiser's clothing bag is booked with your registration and costs 5 €: let us know when you book. Without it, you get a poncho at the finish to keep warm.

Can I bring a non-running companion?

Of course, with the companion rate without a bib: just ask us for the quote.

If I travel alone, do I have to pay for a single room?

Only if you want one, with the supplement shown in the price list; otherwise we pair you with another runner.

What if I get injured before departure?

Basic medical insurance is included; cancellation cover for injury and illness up to the day of departure can be added (you'll find it in the price list).

When do I pay?

When you book we tell you the deposit and the balance deadline.
