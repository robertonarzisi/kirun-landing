Welcome to Copenhagen

A World Championships open to everyone

In 2026 the «classic» Copenhagen Half Marathon takes a break: in its place, on 19 and 20 September, the city hosts the World Athletics Road Running Championships Copenhagen 26, the World Championships in road running over three distances: one mile, 5 km and half marathon. What makes it special is that recreational runners race right behind the elite: around 65,000 participants over the weekend, 35,000 in Sunday's half marathon alone.

The course is the same as the 2024 Copenhagen Half Marathon, already tested by nearly 30,000 runners: flat, fast and packed with spectators, with power zones and music along the way. Your race also counts as a stage of the SuperHalfs series.

In this guide you will find the practical information to organise your weekend: hotel, bib collection, start groups, course, what happens after the finish and a mini guide to the city. Times are those published by the organiser as of 8 September: always check the official links in the side panel, where the organiser updates rules and logistics right up to race day.

The weekend at a glance

The four-day programme

  1. Fri 18
    Arrival and bib collection

    From the airport take Metro M2 to Kongens Nytorv, then a 5-minute walk. Rooms are ready from 15:00; if you arrive earlier you can leave your luggage in the hotel lockers.

    The Expo at Forum is open until 19:00: Friday afternoon is the best moment to collect your bib without rushing. The KiRun staff will be waiting at the hotel and will let you know meeting times and points.

  2. Sat 19
    Shakeout run and World Championships to watch

    Morning: 20–30 minutes of easy running (Kastellet and Langelinie are one kilometre from the hotel). At 10:10 and 10:30 the one mile world finals start on Østerbrogade, between Trianglen and Parken stadium; at 14:10 and 14:40 the 5 km finals. If you still need your bib, the Expo closes at 18:00.

    Evening: early, carb-based dinner and get your bib, race bag and kit ready.

  3. Sun 20
    Race day

    Breakfast at the hotel, then Metro M3 from Kongens Nytorv to Trianglen (bag drop) or Vibenshus Runddel (straight to your start group). Runners Area opens at 7:30. Join your start group 10–15 minutes before the time shown in the start group table. After the finish, KiRun meeting point in Runners Area, as communicated by the staff. The afternoon is yours.

  4. Mon 21
    Departure

    Check-out by 11:00. If your flight is in the afternoon, leave your luggage in the hotel lockers and enjoy your last hours in the city.

Arriving on Saturday?

The Expo closes at 18:00 on Saturday and on Sunday only emergency bib collection is available in Runners Area, with queues. Plan your arrival so you can get to Forum by Saturday afternoon: from Kongens Nytorv it is two Metro stops (M1 or M2 towards Vanløse, Forum station).

Where you stay

The hotel and how to get there

Your stay is at the Wakeup Copenhagen Borgergade, Borgergade 9, 1300 København K: a modern, functional hotel in the heart of the city, steps away from the Nyhavn canal, the royal palace of Amalienborg and the Strøget shopping street. Rooms are compact with a minimalist design, air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and TV; the reception is open 24 hours and the breakfast room is bright and welcoming. It is the ideal base for combining the race with exploring the city on foot.

Its strength is the location: Kongens Nytorv station, where lines M1, M2, M3 and M4 meet, is a 5-minute walk away. From there you reach the airport, the Expo and the start area in minutes.

  • Wakeup Copenhagen Borgergade – official website

From the airport to the hotel

  • By Metro: from Terminal 3 follow the signs to the Metro and take the M2 towards Vanløse to Kongens Nytorv (about 15 minutes, trains every few minutes, also at night). From there the hotel is a 5-minute walk: exit towards Store Kongensgade and turn into Borgergade. Total 20–25 minutes. You need a 3-zone ticket, from the machines or the DOT Tickets app.
  • By taxi: 20–25 minutes depending on traffic; cards accepted.

Check-in, check-out and luggage storage

  • Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00.
  • The hotel provides lockers in two sizes: the small ones fit a cabin-size suitcase. The price depends on the size, payment is by card directly at the terminal (no cash) and no receipt is issued.
  • Breakfast is a buffet. We will confirm Sunday's opening time on site: if you want to eat earlier, keep something tried and tested in your room (bread, banana, energy bar).

The Expo and bib collection

There is the ASICS area with the official Championships merchandise (available only in Copenhagen), brand stands, a refreshment point with coffee and juices, and High5 products. Entry is free for non-runners too.

The race pack

Your race pack contains the bib with integrated timing chip, the official ASICS World Championships T-shirt in the size given at registration and the race bag for Sunday's bag drop. You receive the finisher medal at the finish; photos, finisher video and digital diploma will be available online after the race.

  • Bring an ID document and your registration confirmation email (the organiser writes from noreply@njuko.com): showing it on your phone is fine. If you have not received anything, write to us before you travel.
  • Half marathon bibs can be collected on every Expo opening day. The organiser recommends avoiding peak times: Friday afternoon after check-in or Saturday at opening time are the quietest slots.
  • If you really cannot make it to the Expo, emergency bib collection is available on Sunday in Runners Area: expect queues and arrive well in advance.
  • The organiser warns that for some late half marathon registrations the official T-shirt may not be available.
  • Your bib shows the colour and letter of your start group: check them straight away.
Sunday 20 September

Race day

Start and finish are in the Østerbro district, around Fælledparken: the start blocks are on Jagtvej and Østerbrogade, the finish on Øster Allé next to Parken stadium. The logistical heart is the Runners Area inside the park: bag drop, toilets, meeting point, refreshments and the final party. It is open on Sunday from 7:30 to 16:00 (and Saturday 7:30 to 18:00) and entry is free for supporters too.

Getting there from the hotel

  • By Metro: from Kongens Nytorv take the M3 Cityringen (towards Østerport/Trianglen). Get off at Trianglen if you need to drop your bag in Runners Area (3 stops, then a 5-minute walk), or at Vibenshus Runddel to go straight to your start group. About 20 minutes in total. New for 2026, the Event Ticket gives you 12 hours of unlimited public transport in the Rejsebillet app.
  • On foot: about 30–35 minutes (3 km) along Store Kongensgade and Østerbrogade. A good walking warm-up, weather permitting.

Bag drop

Use the official race bag from your race pack and hand it in at the Runners Area bag drop before heading to your start group. Storage for backpacks or bags other than the official race bag is available for a fee. If you wear an old sweater to the start to take off at the last minute, leave it in the start area: it is collected and donated.

Start groups and pace teams

The start is in waves: your group depends on the expected finish time given at registration and is shown on your bib with a colour and a letter. The groups are colour-coded blocks in the start area and move towards the line one at a time over two hours: join your group 10–15 minutes before its expected start time, not earlier, so you do not stand around getting cold. Water and toilets are available in the start area. Your official time is your net time: it starts when you cross the start line.

Every group has pacers, recognisable by coloured balloons with the target finish time printed on them: from 1:25 to 2:30, every 5 minutes. No sign-up needed: line up behind the balloon that matches your goal and let them carry you. The time limit is 3 hours from when you cross the start line. Running with headphones is not forbidden but strongly discouraged: you must be able to hear the race officials.

Night-before checklist
  • Bib fixed to your shirt (pins or magnets), chip integrated: do not fold it.
  • Shoes and socks already used in training; layered clothing, an old top to leave at the start.
  • Gels or nutrition you have already tested: no experiments on race day.
  • Race bag with what you need afterwards: dry shirt, jacket, something to eat.
  • ID, payment card, charged phone, Metro ticket or Event Ticket already in the app.
  • Alarm set according to your start group: hotel to Runners Area takes 20 minutes plus the bag drop.
21.0975 km

The course

The official distance is 21.0975 km, on a flat, fast course through the historic centre and Copenhagen's «bridge districts». It is the same course as the 2024 Copenhagen Half Marathon, starting and finishing at Fælledparken. In short, following the official map:

  • km 0–2 · Start on Jagtvej, first kilometres along Østerbrogade and through Trianglen, in the heart of Østerbro.
  • km 3–4 · Towards Østerport and the Kastellet, the star-shaped fortress, skirting the Østre Anlæg gardens.
  • km 5–8 · Frederiksstaden and the waterfront: Amalienborg, the Marble Church and back towards Nyhavn and Kongens Nytorv. This is the hotel's neighbourhood: your supporters can cheer here, five minutes' walk from the room.
  • km 9–10 · Slotsholmen with Christiansborg Palace and the old town, up to the area of Rådhuspladsen and the Tivoli Gardens.
  • km 11–14 · Vesterbro and Frederiksberg, the most residential stretch, along long tree-lined avenues.
  • km 15–17 · Nørrebro, the city's youngest and most multicultural district.
  • km 18–19 · The Lakes (Søerne) and Dronning Louises Bro, one of the most photographed spots of the race.
  • km 20–21 · Back towards Østerbro and the final straight on Øster Allé, with a wall of spectators and Parken stadium in the background.
  • Refreshment stations along the course and at the finish with water and energy drink; test in training what you will use on race day and check the official website for the products served.
  • Power zones, DJs and music at several points of the course: Copenhagen's crowd is among the warmest in Europe.
  • Timing is by chip: your time starts when you cross the start line and stops at the finish (net time).
  • Official course map (PDF)With kilometre markers, start and finish.

After the finish line

The finish and afterwards

  • Collect your official World Championships finisher medal and the finish-line refreshments.
  • Set a personal best? Ring the PR bell in Runners Area: that is what it is there for.
  • Pick up your bag at the same bag drop where you left it.
  • SuperHalfs: your Copenhagen result is automatically added to your SuperPassport within a few days. If this is your sixth race, the SuperMedal awaits you in the SuperHalfs Area inside Runners Area.
  • Photos, finisher video and digital diploma are included in your entry and go online after the race.
  • In Runners Area you will find refreshments, bars, the partner area and the official Championships merchandise (while stocks last). The party goes on until 16:00.
KiRun meeting point

After the finish we all meet in Runners Area, at the spot the staff tells you at the hotel: group photo, a toast and back together by Metro or on foot, for those with legs left.

Saturday 19 September

Championships Saturday

Saturday is the day of the other two world finals, and they are a show not to miss: the one mile is run on Østerbrogade, with the Trianglen power zone and the finish in front of Parken stadium, the 5 km in the afternoon. The mass starts follow the elite by a few minutes, so the streets are in party mode all day.

KiRun tip: the day before, 20–30 minutes of easy running with a few strides is plenty. Then walk little, eat early and simple, and get everything ready for the morning.

Beyond the race

Mini guide to Copenhagen

Getting around

  • Metro: fast, clean and running 24/7. Lines M1, M2, M3 (Cityringen) and M4 connect the main districts and the airport. Kongens Nytorv, 5 minutes from the hotel, is the hub of all four.
  • S-tog (urban trains) for the suburbs and nearby towns; frequent buses where the Metro does not reach.
  • Havnebus: the yellow public-transport ferries linking the neighbourhoods along the harbour, with a standard ticket.
  • Bike: Copenhagen is the city of bicycles. Cycle lanes are everywhere and rentals are a tap away in the bike-sharing apps; during Championships weekend it is the organiser's recommended way to get around.

Tickets are sold at the machines in Metro and train stations, at 7-Eleven kiosks or in the DOT Tickets app, the most convenient option. For race day there is the official Event Ticket: 12 hours of unlimited travel in the Rejsebillet app. The Copenhagen Card includes unlimited public transport and entry to many attractions: worth it if you plan several museums.

Three places not to miss

Nyhavn

The colourful harbour three minutes from the hotel: a stroll, a drink on the canal or a boat tour through the canals. On Sunday it is also a great cheering spot, around km 8.

The Little Mermaid and Kastellet

On the Langelinie waterfront, the statue inspired by Andersen's tale is small but iconic. The Kastellet, the star-shaped fortress with its windmill, is perfect for Saturday's shakeout run.

Tivoli

The historic amusement park (since 1843) next to the central station: magical at night with lights, gardens and shows. Check the season's opening hours.

If you still have time: the Rundetaarn, the 17th-century round tower with its spiral ramp and rooftop views; Torvehallerne, the covered market at Nørreport for breakfast or lunch (10 minutes' walk from the hotel); the canals of Christianshavn.

Two souvenirs and one dish

  • Royal Copenhagen porcelain: Danish elegance and tradition, ideal as a gift.
  • Scandinavian design: lamps, home accessories and minimalist objects. For an edible souvenir, gourmet liquorice.
  • Smørrebrød: the famous open-faced rye bread sandwich, topped with salmon, herring, roast beef or shrimp. For after the race, a kanelsnegl (cinnamon roll) and a Danish hot dog never disappoint.
Before you travel

Practical information for Denmark

Documents
EU citizens enter with a valid national ID card or passport. No visa is required for tourist stays. Travellers from other countries: check the entry requirements for Denmark.
Currency
Danish krone (DKK), 1 € ≈ 7.45 DKK. Cards are accepted everywhere, even for small amounts: you will hardly need any cash.
Language
Danish; English is spoken and understood by everyone.
Electricity
Type C and K sockets, 230 V / 50 Hz. Most continental European plugs work without an adapter; travellers from the UK, Ireland and outside Europe need one.
Phone
Country code +45. EU SIM cards roam at no extra cost («Roam like at home»).
Time zone
Central European Time (CET/CEST), UTC+2 in September.
Weather
In September temperatures range on average from 11 to 17 °C, with wind and rain possible: bring a windproof layer for before and after the race. On 20 September the sun rises around 6:55 and sets around 19:25.
Emergencies
112 for police, ambulance and fire brigade. For non-urgent medical issues, the Capital Region's medical helpline is 1813. 24-hour pharmacy: Steno Apotek, Vesterbrogade 6C, opposite the central station.
Water and tipping
Tap water is excellent, fill your bottle. Tipping is not expected: service is included.
