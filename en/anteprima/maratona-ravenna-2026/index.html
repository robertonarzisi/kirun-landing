The trip

KiRun Ravenna package

Ravenna is a UNESCO heritage site and its marathon passes by all eight protected monuments. On Sunday, November 8, the marathon, half marathon, and 10 km Good Morning Ravenna will run together: start from Pala De André, finish at Museo MAR in the city center.

On November 7 and 8: bus departure from Biella on Saturday morning, one night in a hotel in Ravenna with breakfast, race on Sunday, and return to Biella the same day. You choose the distance, the bib number is added to the travel fee.

What's covered

Included & not included

The package includes

  • Round trip bus from Biella
  • 1 night at Hotel Cube Ravenna, 4 stars, in a double room
  • Breakfast
  • Room sharing with another runner, free and guaranteed, for those traveling alone
  • Assistance from KiRun staff throughout the weekend

Not included

  • Race bib: not included in the fee; added based on the chosen distance, as per the services table
  • Single room: upon request, with a supplement as per the services table
  • Lunches, dinners, and drinks
  • Everything not mentioned under the included section
Where you stay

Hotel Cube Ravenna 4*

Hotel Cube Ravenna, 4 stars, located at Via Luigi Masotti 2 in Ravenna. Overnight stay in a double room with breakfast.

Traveling alone? You can choose to share a double room with another runner from the group, free and guaranteed, or a single room, upon request and with a supplement.

How it works

The programme

Saturday, November 7: meeting in Biella at the parking lot of Stadio Lamarmora, pool side, and bus departure at 7:00 AM. Arrival in Ravenna, check-in at Hotel Cube and bib collection at the Expò Marathon Village at Pala De André. Afternoon and evening free in the city center, among San Vitale, Galla Placidia, and the mosaics.

Sunday, November 8: breakfast at the hotel and transfer to the start at Pala De André. At 9:15 AM, the marathon and half marathon start, at 10:15 AM the 10 km Good Morning Ravenna. Finish for everyone at Museo MAR, in the city center. In the afternoon, after the race, bus departure for Biella: return expected around 8:00 PM.

How the booking is confirmed

The reservation is confirmed with the signing of the contract and the payment of the deposit. Accompanying persons who do not run pay the same fee as the package, without a bib number.

Deposit and balance

Deposit within 3 days of booking confirmation: €50 per person plus the cost of the chosen bib number. Balance due by 15 October 2026.

Cancellation penalties

The fees paid (package, bib, and supplements) are non-refundable in case of participant withdrawal. Full refund remains if the trip is not confirmed due to failure to reach the minimum number of participants.

Minimum number of participants

The trip is guaranteed upon reaching 30 participants by September 30, 2026. If the minimum number is not reached, KiRun will promptly notify all participants and fully refund the amounts paid.
