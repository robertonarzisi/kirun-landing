The trip

KiRun Smart package

Valencia is famous for its mild climate, flat course, and welcoming atmosphere: the ideal half marathon to set your personal record.

From October 23 to 26: three nights in the city with breakfast, early breakfast on race day, dedicated transfer to and from the start area, and the KiRun desk in the hotel always available.

What's covered

Included & not included

The package includes

  • 3-night stay with breakfast in a 3* hotel
  • Medical-luggage insurance
  • Assistance from our staff during the event
  • Dedicated KiRun desk in the hotel
  • Early breakfast on race day
  • Dedicated transfer on race day
  • KiRun Orange tank top
  • KiRun group training program

Not included

  • Airfare: not included; upon request, we prepare a personalized quote
  • Race bib: extra service, not included in the fee; if requested, it is charged separately as per the services table
  • Cancellation insurance: optional service, not included in the fee; if requested, it is charged separately as per the services table
  • Premium package and extra nights: upon request, as per the services table
Where you stay

Holiday Inn Ciudad de las Ciencias

Holiday Inn Ciudad de las Ciencias 3*: located in front of the City of Arts and Sciences, offers a comfortable stay with services designed for half marathon runners. For maximum convenience on race day, KiRun organizes a dedicated transfer service to and from the event area. The hotel features a seasonal outdoor pool, ideal for relaxing after the run.

If you desire a higher-level facility or additional options, please let us know: we will be happy to suggest alternatives.
