The trip

KiRun Smart package

The route of the Prague Half Marathon runs along the Vltava River and through the historic center: flat, fast, with the city around you for all 21 km. In Prague the race is run on a Saturday, not on a Sunday. KiRun is the official tour operator of the SuperHalfs circuit.

From April 2 to 5: three nights in Prague with breakfast, assistance from our staff on-site, KiRun tank top, and the group training schedule to get you ready for the start.

Where you stay

B&B Hotel Prague City 3*

B&B Hotel Prague City 3*: a practical and functional choice for those participating in the Prague Half Marathon. It is located in the Florenc area, a short distance from the historic center, and allows easy access to both the start and finish areas of the race as well as the main points of interest, such as the Old Town Square and Charles Bridge. Well connected by public transport, it is the ideal option for those seeking essential comfort and a great location.

Clarion Hotel Prague Old Town 4* (supplement as per the services table): privileged location close to the historic center and just a few minutes' walk from the start area. From here, you can easily reach the Old Town Square, the Vltava River, and the most picturesque streets of the center. Perfect for those who wish to combine the race with an elegant stay in the heart of Prague.

If you desire a higher-level structure or additional options, please let us know: we will be happy to propose alternatives.
